# 19 Mandate of Heaven — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier FULL. Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Dunhuang Star Chart** (British Library Or.8210/S.3326): the oldest complete preserved star atlas; Tang dynasty or slightly later (dating debated; 7th–10th century). Use as a style source only, not as evidence of Han practice. Verified (IDP article).
- **Han material culture:** TLV (liubo) bronze mirrors (cosmograms), lacquerware, Mawangdui silk texts (including the "Prognostications of the Five Planets" and comet chart, 2nd century BCE; Hunan Provincial Museum). Secondary.
- **Instruments:** later armillary spheres (Ming copies at Purple Mountain Observatory, Nanjing); gnomons (Dengfeng Observatory, Yuan). Secondary.
- **Palette:** lacquer red and black, bronze green patina, silk ivory, night indigo, star gold.
- **Typography:** a clear sans with CJK support (e.g., Source Han Sans) for hanzi; clerical-script style for titles only if designed by a calligrapher.
- **Materials:** bamboo slips bound with cord, silk, cast bronze, lacquer.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Deduction and hidden models:** Mastermind, Zendo, and Black Box (inferring hidden rules); Return of the Obra Dinn (records reasoning).
- **Prediction markets/wagers:** Wits & Wagers.
- **Design note:** the sky is a hidden model; players learn by observation, mirroring the astronomer's task.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- Han court astronomy: the Taishi ling (Grand Astrologer) office maintained calendars and recorded omens; astrology was political ("astrology for an empire"), tied to the Mandate of Heaven. Verified (Pankenier, INSAP VII paper).
- 28 xiu (lunar lodges) as the equatorial coordinate system. Verified.
- Eclipse prediction in Han: lunar eclipse cycles were known; solar eclipse prediction was limited. Verified (Pankenier summary; Secondary for details).
- Sensitivities: ancestral rites and imperial ritual; avoid treating them as exploitable magic. Avoid "inscrutable East" tropes.

## 6. Audio and Music Direction
- Han-era instruments (guqin, se, bianzhong bells, sheng) in original compositions; night insects, water clock drips; court drums for omens. Avoid imitating ritual music as gameplay.
