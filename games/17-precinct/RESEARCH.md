# 17 Precinct — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier NONE (fictional city). Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Risograph printing:** stencil duplicator printing (Riso Kagaku, 1980s) with spot-colour soy inks; fluorescent pink, teal, yellow, blue, and orange are standard Riso drum colours. Visible misregistration and grain are part of the look. Secondary.
- **Political ephemera:** 20th-century campaign posters and union handbills (Library of Congress Prints and Photographs, Center for the Study of Political Graphics). Reference only; no real slogans.
- **Palette:** Riso fluorescent pink `#FF48B0`, teal `#00838A`, yellow `#FFE800`, blue `#0078BF`, orange `#FF6C2F` on newsprint `#F4F0E6`.
- **Typography:** grotesque display (e.g., a Franklin Gothic-like face) and a typewriter face for poll memos.
- **Materials:** newsprint, overprint, halftone dots, stapled zines.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Area control:** El Grande; **election games:** 1960: The Making of the President (Jason Matthews and Christian Leonhard, 2007), Campaign Manager 2008; Twilight Struggle for influence placement.
