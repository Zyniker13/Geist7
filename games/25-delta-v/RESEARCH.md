## 1. Art Direction References
- **Mission graphics:** NASA and ESA mission patches, trajectory diagrams, and JPL's "Visions of the Future" posters (NASA/JPL-Caltech, public). Secondary.
- **Engineering drawing:** blueprint/diazo aesthetics (whiteprint process). Secondary.
- **Palette:** blueprint blue `#1B3A5C`, diazo violet-blue lines, safety orange, cryogenic frost white, Mars ochre.
- **Typography:** a technical monospace for telemetry and a condensed grotesque for labels (NASA "worm"-era vibe without copying logos).
