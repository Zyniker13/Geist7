# 23 Stratum — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier NONE (fictional sites). Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Archaeological drawing:** section and plan drawings in excavation reports (Museum of London Archaeology single-context recording manuals); Munsell soil colour charts for context colours. Secondary.
- **Palette:** Munsell-like soil hues (10YR browns, 2.5Y olives, 5YR reds), chalk white, charcoal black, survey-tape yellow.
- **Typography:** technical sans for labels, handwritten-style annotation font for recording sheets.
- **Materials:** permatrace drawing film, trowel-scraped soil textures, finds bags with labels.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Harris matrix** (Edward C. Harris, 1973; "Principles of Archaeological Stratigraphy", 1979): a directed graph of stratigraphic relations. Secondary.
- **Deduction games:** Return of the Obra Dinn, Her Story, and Minesweeper-like inference; Heaven's Vault for archaeology tone.
- **Design note:** destruction is irreversible — excavation erases what it reveals, the core tension.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- Excavation ethics: human remains and descendant communities (e.g., NAGPRA in the US). The game excludes burials entirely (§11).
- Avoid: treasure-hunting framing, looting glamour, metal-detecting for loot.

## 4. Candidate Advisor Types and Institutions
