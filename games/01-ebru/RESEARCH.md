# 01 Ebru — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier FULL. Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **UNESCO:** "Ebru, Turkish art of marbling" inscribed on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2014. Verified.
- **Masters and lineages (reference only; no living masters' names in-game without consent):** Hatip Mehmed Efendi (18th century, hatip ebrusu), Necmeddin Okyay (1883–1976, floral ebru), Mustafa Düzgünman (1920–1990). Secondary.
- **Collections:** Topkapı Palace Museum library, Istanbul University Rare Books Library, Süleymaniye Library (ebru papers in manuscripts), Library of Congress and V&A marbled papers. Secondary.
- **Palette:** natural earth pigments (ochre, umber, indigo, rose madder, chrome green) on cream paper; avoid neon.
- **Typography:** humanist serif with full Turkish support (ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü).
- **Materials:** tekne (tray), gum tragacanth (kitre) size, ox gall (öd), horsehair brushes on rose-wood handles, awls (biz), combs (tarak).

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Mathematical marbling:** Jaffer, Lu, and others modelled marbling strokes as area-preserving fluid transforms (tine lines, vortices). Secondary.
- **Puzzle lineage:** pattern-matching puzzles (I Love Hue, Kami) and colour-mixing games.
- **Design note:** strokes are irreversible like real ebru; preview is the teaching tool.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- Ebru developed in the Islamic world (Persian and Ottoman) from the 15th–16th centuries; used for book arts, calligraphy borders, and official documents (anti-forgery). Secondary.
- Living practice: practised in Türkiye and taught in workshops; the UNESCO listing emphasises master–apprentice transmission. Verified.
- Sensitivities: no calligraphic religious text in borders; no claim to teach ebru as a craft replacement; credit the tradition.

## 4. Candidate Advisor Types and Institutions
- Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Culture and Tourism (ICH section).
- Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality Art and Vocational Training Courses (İSMEK; now İstanbul Sanat ve Meslek Eğitimi) traditional arts teachers.
- Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University, Traditional Turkish Arts department.
- Altın El Sanatları (craft heritage organisation, Verified page on ebru).

## 6. Audio and Music Direction
- Ney, kanun, and oud idioms in original compositions; water and brush foley. Avoid Sufi ritual music (sema) as background.
