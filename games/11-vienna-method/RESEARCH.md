# 11 Vienna Method — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier NONE (labelled history, no living tradition). Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Isotype (International System of Typographic Picture Education):** developed by Otto Neurath, Marie Neurath (née Reidemeister), and Gerd Arntz at the Gesellschafts- und Wirtschaftsmuseum in Wien from the mid-1920s (originally called the "Vienna Method of Pictorial Statistics"). Secondary.
- **Collections:** the Otto and Marie Neurath Isotype Collection at the University of Reading (donated 1971); Gerd Arntz holdings also at Reading; the Isotype Revisited project (AHRC, 2007–2011). Verified.
- **Key works:** "Gesellschaft und Wirtschaft" atlas (1930); "Modern Man in the Making" (1939). Secondary.
- **Palette:** Isotype's limited flat colours: red `#C8102E`-like, blue, black, ochre, green on off-white; no gradients.
- **Typography:** Futura (Paul Renner, 1927) was used in Isotype charts. Use Futura PT or a licensed geometric sans.
- **Materials:** linocut-like crisp pictograms, offset-printed paper.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **SimCity** and civic simulators; **Frostpunk** for needs pressure; **Mini Metro** for minimalist information design.
- **Information design theory:** Neurath's rules (repetition not scaling, consistent symbols, "words divide, pictures unite"); Tufte's data-ink.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- Red Vienna (1919–1934): municipal housing (Gemeindebauten such as Karl-Marx-Hof) and welfare. Political history; the game sets scenarios in 1920s Vienna with labelled history and avoids partisan framing. Secondary.
- Neurath emigrated after 1934 (Netherlands, then England). Sensitivity: interwar politics and the 1934 civil war; avoid partisan iconography.
