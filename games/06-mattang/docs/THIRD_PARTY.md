# Mattang — Third-Party Components

None. Mattang uses only Apple frameworks (Foundation, SwiftUI, SpriteKit, Metal, AVFoundation, XCTest) and the repo's own code. All art and audio is procedural and asset-free. No third-party packages, fonts, or assets are bundled; dependencies stay on the `_CONVENTIONS.md` §4.4 allow-list (currently: none).

If a third-party component is ever added, list it here with its license (CC0 or another permissive license compatible with a commercial, closed-source app), its source, and its attribution text, and record the decision in docs/DECISIONS.md.
