# 24 Patang — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier FULL. Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Uttarayan in Ahmedabad and Surat:** rooftop (dhabu) culture, pol houses of old Ahmedabad (a UNESCO World Heritage City since 2017), kite markets (Patang Bazaar, Raipur Darwaja). Secondary.
- **Kite craft:** tissue-paper fighter kites with bamboo spine and bow (kamaan); named types such as mynah and kagdi. Verified (Mint/allaboutgujarat).
- **Kite museum:** Patang Kite Museum, Sanskar Kendra, Ahmedabad. Secondary.
- **Palette:** saturated tissue colours (magenta, marigold, turquoise, leaf green), January haze sky, sandstone and lime-plaster rooftops.
- **Typography:** rounded sans with Gujarati script support (e.g., Noto Sans Gujarati) for glossary; no faux-Devanagari Latin.
- **Materials:** tissue paper, bamboo, cotton string on wooden firki, terrace parapets.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Fighter kite flying** in Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, and Pakistan (Basant, now banned in Lahore over manja deaths), and Afghanistan (gudiparan bazi). Secondary.
- **Physics duel games:** Toribash, Getting Over It (physics as skill); kite physics modelled with lift/drag tables.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- **Uttarayan / Makar Sankranti** (14 January; Vasi Uttarayan 15 January) marks the sun's northward movement; in Gujarat it is famous for kite flying; also has religious observances (sacred bathing, charity). Verified (dates, allaboutgujarat).
- **Harm:** glass-coated and "Chinese" synthetic manja injures and kills birds and people. Karuna Abhiyan (Gujarat Forest Department, launched 2017; runs about 10–20 January) treated 16,380 birds in 2026, with 14,690 surviving; pigeons were about 83%. Verified (Times of India, DD News). Jivdaya Charitable Trust runs bird hospitals. Verified (reporting).
- Sensitivities: religious rituals not as gameplay; no caricatured Gujarati accents; no glass manja even as an "evil" option.

## 6. Audio and Music Direction
- Rooftop soundscape: shouts, film songs on speakers (licensed or original in the style), drums (dhol), string hiss on firki. Avoid devotional music as background.
