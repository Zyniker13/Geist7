## Rosette Game Rules

*Copied from the game roadmap §4.*

### Overview
Rosette is a tavern circuit of ancient board games. The player competes in a series of games drawn from Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Mesoamerican, and other ancient traditions. Each game is scored on skill, and the tavern circuit ranks players by total score.

### Core Mechanics
- **Game selection:** The circuit presents a sequence of games. Some are fixed; others are drawn from a pool based on player level.
- **Scoring:** Each game produces a score based on skillful play. The final tavern ranking is the sum of all scores.
- **Replay:** Every game can be replayed from a save file or viewed as a replay.

### Scoring
- **Victory margin:** Bonus points for winning by a large margin.
- **Streak bonus:** Bonus points for consecutive wins.
- **Difficulty modifier:** Harder games (more AI levels, fewer hints) multiply the base score.

### Accessibility
- All gameplay is visible and audible; no visual-only or audio-only information.
- Color-blind safe palette.
- Adjustable text size.
- Haptic feedback toggleable.

### Content Tiers
- **Attested:** Rules and content derived from verified historical sources.
- **Reconstructed:** Rules and content derived from scholarly reconstructions.
- **Invented:** New content designed to fit the theme and gameplay style.

### Privacy
- No network communication.
- No telemetry.
- All data stored locally.
