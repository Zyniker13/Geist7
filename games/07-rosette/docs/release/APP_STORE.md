## Description

Rosette is the Royal Game of Ur played in a smoky tavern circuit, where the
dice are fair but the stakes are not.

**The board is ancient.** Seven pieces each, twenty squares, four binary
tetrahedral dice — the ruleset reconstructed from the 3rd-millennium sets
excavated at Ur. Rosette squares grant an extra roll and safety; a landing on
an opponent sends their piece all the way home. Bear all seven off the far
end to win the match.

**The stakes are modern.** Before each match, set a stake. Mid-match, offer
the doubling cube — as in backgammon — or play an omen token, a one-shot bend
of the rules bought between matches. Grow a purse across the circuit, and win
the final tavern's tournament.

**Two rulesets.** Play the modern Reconstructed ruleset, or the Tablet
ruleset that follows only what the Babylonian tablet of Itti-Marduk-balāṭu
(BM 33333B, 177–176 BCE) attests: five pieces, astragal dice, fortunes on the
squares. The game labels each rule Reconstructed or Attested.

**The circuit.** Tenochtitlan to Thebes and back through Ur's taverns: each
stop adds a historical variant — Finkel's tablet rules, the Masters' long
route, a Senet-like race (Reconstructed), and Patolli on a 52-space cross.
Opponents have personalities, from careful to reckless, and get stronger
deeper into the circuit.

**Ways to play.** Solo against the AI in the circuit, local pass-and-play on
one device, and async online matches (added after launch). No account needed
for the core game.

**Made for iPad first,** with full keyboard, pointer, and Dynamic Type
support; the board renders at 60 frames per second on every supported device.
