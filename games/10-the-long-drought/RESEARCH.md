# 10 The Long Drought — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier HISTORICAL. Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Mycenaean frescoes:** Pylos (Palace of Nestor) griffin and lyre-player frescoes; Tiryns boar hunt; Knossos for Minoan contrast. Use flat colour fields and wavy borders.
- **Linear B tablets:** clay tablets from Pylos and Knossos (National Archaeological Museum, Athens; Heraklion Archaeological Museum). Use signs as texture only, never as fake translations.
- **Medinet Habu reliefs** (Ramesses III, c. 1175 BCE) depicting the "Sea Peoples": reference for ship silhouettes; avoid copying the triumphal-propaganda framing.
- **Palette:** fresco ochre, Egyptian blue, terracotta, olive, parched straw, sea teal.
- **Typography:** a geometric serif for headers; numerals stylised after Linear B tally strokes for resource counts (decorative only).
- **Materials:** fired clay, painted plaster, bronze, sun-cracked earth for drought states.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **COIN series** (GMT Games; Volko Ruhnke's Andean Abyss, 2012): card-driven asymmetric factions, eligibility, Op + SA, flowchart bots. The Long Drought uses original rules in this genre. Secondary.
- **Pax series** (Phil Eklund) for economic and diplomatic asymmetry.
- **Design theory:** "systems collapse" games (e.g., Pandemic Legacy-style escalation) show pressure tracks; drought is a global clock.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- **Late Bronze Age collapse** (c. 1200–1150 BCE): palatial systems in Greece, the Hittite state, and Ugarit fell; causes debated (drought, earthquakes, migrations, trade disruption, internal revolt). Secondary (Cline 2014/2021; Knapp and Manning 2016).
- **Drought evidence:** Kaniewski et al. (2013) pollen and isotope studies in coastal Syria and Cyprus. Secondary.
- **Sensitivities:** modern Greek, Turkish, Cypriot, Syrian, and Lebanese heritage claims; avoid nationalist mapping. Present the "Sea Peoples" as a contested modern label.

## 6. Audio and Music Direction
- No surviving Mycenaean music; use original compositions with lyre (phorminx), aulos-like reeds, and frame drums, clearly labelled as evocative. Drought states shift to sparse wind and cicadas.
