## 2. Design Lineage
- **Stick charts as models:** Ascher (1995) analysed charts as mathematical models of swell behaviour (reflection, refraction, diffraction around islands). The mattang is an abstract teaching model of swell interaction around one island; meddo shows a few atolls with actual swells; rebbelib shows a whole chain. Verified (Genz et al. 2009; Smithsonian).
- **Wave piloting knowledge:** navigators read swells by feel in the canoe hull; key concepts include dilep (the "backbone" path between islands formed by swell interactions), okar ("root", a line of nodes), and booj ("knot", the node where swells meet). Verified (Genz et al. 2009; Genz 2011).
- **Design theory:** the game turns the physical intuition (islands bend swells; crossings mark paths) into ray tracing; it is an abstraction and must say so.
- **Related games:** none traditional; light-beam puzzle games (e.g., laser-reflection puzzles) are a mechanical relative.

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- **Origins and transmission:** navigation knowledge was held by navigator lineages and controlled by chiefs (iroij); it was transmitted privately; charts were memorised on land and not taken to sea. Verified (Genz et al.).
- **Titles:** ri-meto ("person of the ocean") is a title conferred by an iroij after a test voyage (ruprup jokur, "breaking the shell"). Verified (Genz 2011, Genz 2016). Do not use it for game tiers.
- **Revival:** since 2005 a Marshallese-led revival (Alson Kelen, WAM, University of Hawaiʻi researchers, and Captain Korent Joel of Rongelap lineage) has documented and re-practised wave navigation. Name individuals only as published authors; do not contact.
- **Sacred vs secular:** some navigation knowledge is family-held and protected; general concepts published with community consent (Genz et al.) are appropriate references.
- **Sensitivities and avoid list:** no specific family chart reproduction; no claim to teach navigation; no chiefly titles as ranks; no "primitive" framing; no nuclear-testing imagery as decoration (Bikini and Enewetak testing history is a living trauma).
