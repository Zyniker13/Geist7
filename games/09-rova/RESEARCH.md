# 09 Rova — Art, Design, and Culture Research

Research pass: 15 Nov 2026. Sourcing tier FULL. Status legend: Verified, Secondary, Unverified.

## 1. Art Direction References
- **Antemoro (Antaimoro) paper:** handmade bark paper from Ambalavao with pressed flowers; a living craft. Use as UI texture reference only with permission for photography. Secondary.
- **Lamba textiles:** Malagasy woven cloth (lamba akotifahana silk, Merina) for borders; lamba mena (burial shrouds) must not be used. Secondary.
- **Architecture:** highland Merina houses with red laterite; the Rova of Antananarivo (royal palace compound) appears only in About if the title is approved.
- **Palette:** laterite red `#A4442B`, rice-paddy green `#6E8B3D`, zebu horn `#E8DCC4`, raffia `#C9A66B`, ink black.
- **Typography:** rounded humanist sans; Malagasy uses Latin letters (with ô and occasional diacritics); verify ô.
- **Materials:** carved wooden Fanorona boards, stone pieces vs seed or shell pieces, raffia weaving.

## 2. Design Lineage
- **Fanorona rules as attested:** 9 × 5 board (fanoron-tsivy) with 22 pieces each; approach (manindrona) and withdrawal (misintona) captures; non-capturing moves are paika; chains with no repeated direction or point; captures mandatory. Smaller boards: fanoron-dimy (5 × 5) and fanoron-telo (3 × 3). Verified.
- **Vela:** rematch handicap; previous loser starts, captures one piece per move; previous winner captures nothing and must give a capture each move; ends when the winner is down to 5 pieces. Verified (distad.net; Super Duper Games).
- **Alquerque family:** Fanorona's board extends the alquerque line pattern. Secondary.
- **Solved:** weakly solved as a draw (Schadd et al. 2008). Verified.
- **Run-mode lineage:** Slay the Spire (draft between encounters), Into the Breach (small tactical boards).

## 3. Cultural and Historical Context
- National game of Madagascar, widely played; associated in oral history with the Merina kingdom (an often-repeated story about King Andriantompokoindrindra's succession game is folklore; label Unverified).
- Sacred vs secular: Fanorona is secular. Avoid sikidy (divination with seeds), famadihana (reburial), razana (ancestors), and royal regalia.
- Living communities: Malagasy players, schools, and diaspora clubs; the Fédération Malagasy de Fanorona (Unverified as current name).
- Avoid: exoticising lemurs-and-baobab tourism imagery as shorthand; caricatured Malagasy people.

## 6. Audio and Music Direction
- Valiha (tube zither) and kabosy (small guitar) idioms recorded by Malagasy musicians under licence; avoid ritual music (e.g., tromba possession music). Board clicks on wood and stone.
